What this is

Project Prowess is a chained cipher hunt in the style of Cicada 3301: each solution is the door to the next puzzle, and there is no hint system. It exists to find a small number of people who finish what they start, which is a harder thing to test for than it sounds.

No account, no tracking, no leaderboard. The only record of how far you got is how far you got. If you want the ciphers themselves without the hunt, Cipher Lab is the workbench version.
